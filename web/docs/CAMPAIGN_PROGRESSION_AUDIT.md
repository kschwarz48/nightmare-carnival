# Campaign progression audit (D-103)

Every unlockable, where it comes from, and the rhythm of rewards from a fresh save to the end of Hell.

**The rules that frame it (kept as they are):**

- Surviving a night opens the next map; no hidden quest gates progress (D-98).
- Each map's final boss, beaten at a standard dawn, gives its weapon. Its hidden quest gives the passive that evolves
  that weapon.
- Nightmare and Hell open their maps by winning them on that tier, starting with the Carnival Core.
- Finishing the Ghost Train opens the next tier.
- Hidden-quest solutions are unchanged.

### What the audit found

- **Normal's rhythm is good.** Something meaningful comes every map and every hidden quest: a character, a weapon or a
  passive. Weapons and characters are spread across the four maps rather than bunched at the end.
- **Nightmare and Hell had deserts.** Each tier's progression is four map wins, each with an unlock. In between, the
  hidden quests (25–40 minutes each) and the mid-map bosses gave nothing new on a higher tier: their achievements were
  already earned on Normal. A player could solve all four hidden quests on Nightmare for no reward at all.
- **Late, desirable content?** The tier weapons (Lantern, Chains, Breath) and performers (Watchman, Fire-Eater) are
  late by design: they belong to their tiers. They are specialised rather than universally stronger. D-103 tunes the
  Lantern's and Breath's evolutions up to the pack: they were the weakest evolutions despite unlocking last. The early
  weapons were tuned to stay viable through Hell (`WEAPON_BALANCE_AUDIT.md`).
- **No clusters.** Each major milestone grants one or two things at once, never a flood.

### What changed (D-103)

- **Eight new optional achievements** for a map's hidden quest solved on Nightmare (1,200–1,500 LT each) or Hell
  (2,000–2,500 LT each), once each. They fill the deserts without making the quests mandatory. Normal keeps its quests
  as the source of the passives, and the higher tiers still open by winning.
- **The tier shelves gained core-stat ranks.** Every higher-tier run now has something worthwhile to buy afterward,
  as well as the conditional perks (`LOST_AND_FOUND_ECONOMY.md`).
- **Higher-tier nights pay more:** Greed +75% and +150%, from +50% and +100%.

### Save compatibility

- **Nothing to migrate.** No requirement moved, so no earned unlock or purchase can be lost. There are new perk and
  achievement ids, a payout rate, and weapon behaviour.
- **The new hidden-quest achievements** are earned the next time a quest is solved on that tier. A save that solved
  one on Nightmare before D-103 doesn't receive it retroactively: the save never recorded which tier a quest was solved
  on. It costs nothing to earn again.
- **Older saves are untouched:** their perk levels and Lost Tickets stay as they were. The new perks start at rank 0,
  and the save format and version are unchanged.
