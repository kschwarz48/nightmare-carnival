# Balance playtest guide (D-103)

The simulations rule out extremes. People settle the rest. This guide lists what to try, what to watch, and the
questions only real players can answer.

**Testing tips:**

- `?unlock=midway` opens the Midway on a fresh save.
- The Lost & Found shows each tier's shelf once the tier is open.
- C opens the build sheet: weapon levels, qualities, stats.
- M toggles the damage meter, which shows each weapon's share.

## Weapons

**Try the reworked weapons first,** each for a whole night, built around them:

- **Snow-Cone Machine and Blizzard.** Do the slush puddles and roaming storms feel like your choice? Is freezing
  readable?
- **Hot-Dog Grill.** Does kiting through your own grease trail feel good, or punishing when you have to stand and fight?
- **Whack-a-Mole Mallet.** Do the whacks feel heavy? Is the daze noticeable without feeling like a stun-lock?
- **Strongman Bell and High Striker.** Do fissures read as cracks in the ground? Do the High Striker's jackpots feel
  like jackpots?
- **Crystal Orbs and Cosmic.** Beside Tarot or Throwing Cards, does the meter now show the orbs doing a real share? It
  should be about a quarter to a third. Is the swelling orbit visible?

**Then ask:**

- Do formerly weak weapons feel worth picking: Chains, Calliope, the Lantern's and Breath's evolutions, Orbs?
- Are any weapons still automatic picks? Watch whether Tarot plus Throwing Cards stays the default. The Death Card is
  still the best boss killer by design, trimmed about 12%.
- Do the evolutions feel like transformations, especially Blizzard, Whack Frenzy, High Striker and Inferno Grill?
- Which combinations surprised you? Name any weapon that still feels useless beside another.
- Do the Night Express Whistle and Watchman's Lantern still feel as good as before? Their base numbers barely moved:
  the Whistle starts 2 damage lower and gains it back by Lv 4. Last Call now hurts bosses.

## The Lost & Found and the tiers

- **Entering Nightmare** right after finishing Normal: challenging but achievable? Bots can't hold it without the shelf.
  People should be able to with skill.
- **First buys on the new shelves.** Do Phantom Knuckles, Stopped Watch and Grave Broth make you feel stronger after a
  few buys? Do the conditional perks still tempt you?
- **Entering Hell** after finishing Nightmare: a clear step up, not a wall?
- **The grind.** How many Nightmare and Hell nights until the shop feels done? The model says about ten nights a tier
  for the important buys, and a long tail to own everything. Is the tail too long?
- **Fresh guests.** A fresh friend joining a Nightmare or Hell night: clearly underpowered, as intended? Can they still
  help, by reviving, distracting, or racking up kills with the shared XP?

## Unlocks and rewards

- Do the new Nightmare and Hell hidden-quest rewards (1,200–2,500 LT) make the quests worth repeating on those tiers?
- Do players regularly have something new to try? Note any long stretch without a new toy.
- Are any rewards too hard for their value? Milo's and Augustus's challenges should feel earned, not unfair.

## Solo against co-op

- Solo progression: does the jump to Nightmare and Hell feel fair alone?
- Mixed progression in a party (one maxed, one fresh): is the difference visible but not frustrating?
- After a co-op night, does each player receive their own Lost Tickets and achievements, once?
- New-tier quest achievements in co-op: does each player whose save the night counts for receive them?

## The questions only people can answer

1. **Kiting.** Is the Grill's movement-based identity fun, or do players want a forward attack too?
2. **Daze.** Does it change how fights feel, or go unnoticed? Does it ever feel unfair the other way, with too many
   dazed creatures making the night trivial?
3. **The Blizzard's storms.** Do they chase the right things? The bots say they now hunt near you first.
4. **The Nightmare entry.** The model and bots disagree on human difficulty. Where does a good player actually stand
   after finishing Normal?
5. **The grind.** Is the long tail on the tier shelves motivating or tiring?
6. **Remaining overlaps.** The Laser Light Show against the Watchman's Lantern, and their evolutions: do players notice?
